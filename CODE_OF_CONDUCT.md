# Code of Conduct

We welcome participants regardless of background, identity or experience. Be respectful, explain disagreements constructively, give useful technical feedback and credit contributions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where someone represents this project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation proportionately. Reports are assessed fairly and privately; avoid retaliation.

Send confidential conduct reports to alvit.work@gmail.com. Include the relevant public links and enough context to investigate; do not send passwords or unrelated personal data. The maintainer will acknowledge the report and explain next steps while limiting disclosure to what is needed to resolve it.
